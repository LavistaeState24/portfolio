Should you invest in SEO, paid ads or social media first?

It depends on how fast you need results and how long you plan to compete. Paid ads bring traffic as soon as a campaign goes live but stop when the budget stops. SEO takes months to build but keeps bringing visitors without paying per click. Social media builds trust and audience over time. Most growing brands run ads for quick leads while SEO and social compound in the background.

Comparison of paid ads, SEO and social media marketing
Channel Speed of results How you pay Best for
Paid ads Days Per click, lead or view Launches, offers, fast lead flow
SEO Months Content and site work, not per click Steady long-term traffic
Social media Weeks to months Time and content production Trust, community, brand recall

How often should a small business post on social media?

Post as often as you can keep up with good quality — consistency matters more than volume. For most small businesses, a few strong posts a week on one or two platforms beats daily posting that runs out of ideas after a month. Plan content in a monthly calendar, mix educational, behind-the-scenes and offer posts, and review which formats bring saves, shares and enquiries. Our social media team builds these calendars for clients every month.

What makes a website ready for Google and AI answers?

A website is ready for search engines and AI assistants when each page answers a clear question in plain words, loads fast on mobile, and is easy for both people and machines to read. That means descriptive headings, a short direct answer at the top of each section, real text instead of text inside images, and accessible markup that follows the W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. Our web and SEO teams build pages this way from the start.
