What services does Scalixo Media offer?

Scalixo Media offers six core services: social media marketing, performance marketing, SEO and lead generation, website development, branding and identity design, and influencer marketing. You can engage us for a single service or an integrated retainer that combines several. Each project is scoped individually based on goals, timeline, and budget.

How much does a digital marketing project cost?

Project cost at Scalixo Media depends on scope, channels, deliverables, and the level of ongoing management required. Because of that, we quote per project rather than publish fixed rates. Project-based pricing applies to one-time work like websites or branding; monthly retainers cover ongoing marketing, content and ad management. Ad spend and third-party costs are billed separately. Contact us for a scoped quote.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines at Scalixo Media vary by service. Branding and identity projects typically take 3–8 weeks depending on scope. Website development runs 4–10 weeks based on complexity. Marketing retainers start producing results in 2–4 weeks for paid campaigns, and 3–6 months for SEO and organic social. Exact timelines are confirmed in writing before work begins — see our terms for engagement structure.

Which industries has Scalixo Media worked with?

Scalixo Media has worked across real estate, D2C food and beverage, beauty and salon, jewellery, SaaS and CRM platforms, and professional services. Our portfolio includes live websites, ad creative, social content and influencer campaigns spanning these industries. If your industry isn't listed, get in touch — most marketing principles transfer across categories.

Do you work with clients outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Scalixo Media is based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, but works with clients across India and internationally. Most collaboration happens remotely over email, calls and shared workspaces. In-person meetings can be arranged locally when needed. International clients collaborate across time zones with scheduled check-ins. Read our about page for team details.

Who owns the work Scalixo Media creates?

Once agreed fees are paid in full, ownership of final approved deliverables created specifically for you transfers to you. Scalixo Media retains ownership of concepts, drafts, working files and internal frameworks. Third-party assets such as fonts, stock footage and licensed software remain subject to their own licences. Full details are in our terms. Questions about asset delivery? Contact us.
