01 — Why Performance Marketing Matters

Every rupee
has to work.

Scroll

Performance marketing matters because every rupee of ad spend is tied to a measurable action — a lead, a call, a purchase, a registration. Instead of paying for impressions you can't trace to revenue, you pay for outcomes you can see, compare, and optimize. Done right, this makes budget accountable: you learn which channels produce leads, which produce customers, and where spend is being wasted. That's why D2C brands, local service providers, and B2B companies treat performance marketing as a core growth channel — not a line item in the marketing budget.

07 Performance FAQs

Questions before
scaling your ad spend?

Here are the most common questions businesses ask before starting a performance marketing campaign with Scalixo Media.

Ask Your Question

What exactly is performance marketing?

Performance marketing is a results-driven form of paid advertising where you only pay when a specific, measurable action happens—a click, lead, sale, or app install. Unlike brand awareness campaigns, every rupee or dollar spent is tied directly to an outcome, giving you full accountability and control over your return on investment.

Which channels fall under performance marketing?

The most common channels include Paid Search (Google Ads, Bing Ads), Paid Social (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest), Affiliate Marketing, Programmatic Display, and Shopping Ads (Google Shopping, Meta Advantage+). We select and integrate the right mix based on where your audience is most likely to convert.

What is ROAS, and what counts as a “good” number?

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) is revenue generated divided by ad spend. A “good” ROAS depends entirely on your profit margins, not an industry average. A business with 70% margins might thrive at 2x ROAS, while a low-margin business may need 6x or higher just to break even. We set targets based on your specific unit economics.

How much does performance marketing cost?

Agency fees vary based on the number of channels, markets, and the complexity of your tracking setup. Globally, retainers typically range from $2,000 to $20,000+ per month, excluding ad spend. What matters more than the headline fee is the ratio of management cost to media budget—if the fee consumes too much of your spend, most of your money is buying attention rather than customers.

How long does it take for paid ads to work?

Expect roughly two weeks for setup and data gathering, four to six weeks for the platform’s bidding algorithm to stabilise, and a first reliable performance read between week six and week twelve. The timeline is driven by how quickly the account accumulates conversion data—accounts with fewer monthly conversions will naturally sit at the slower end.

Does performance marketing work for B2B and service businesses, or only e-commerce?

It works across industries, but the metrics change. E-commerce brands optimize for ROAS and cost per purchase. B2B companies focus on cost per qualified lead and pipeline contribution. Service businesses track bookings, calls, and form fills. The core principle is the same—pay for a measurable action—but what counts as a “conversion” is defined by your business model.

Will I own my ad accounts and data if we stop working together?

You should own your ad accounts—always. If an agency creates the account inside their own business manager, the conversion history, pixel data, and audiences belong to them and cannot be exported. We build campaigns inside accounts owned by your company, so you retain full ownership of all historical data and creative assets if you ever choose to part ways. For guidance on how native advertising disclosures should be handled, see the FTC’s Native Advertising Guide.
