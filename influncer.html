08 FAQs

Questions before
starting your campaign?

Here are common questions businesses ask before starting their influencer marketing campaign with Scalixo Media.

Ask Your Question

What is influencer marketing?

Influencer marketing is a strategy where brands collaborate with creators to promote products, services, offers, or experiences through trusted content.

Do you only work with famous influencers?

No. We shortlist suitable creators based on your niche, location, audience, content quality, budget, and campaign goal.

Does influencer marketing work for local businesses?

Yes. Influencer marketing works well for salons, cafes, restaurants, real estate, fashion, beauty, healthcare, education, events, e-commerce, and local businesses.

Do you manage creator coordination?

Yes. We manage creator shortlisting, briefing, coordination, content approvals, posting timeline, and performance review.

Can we reuse influencer content?

Yes, influencer content can often be repurposed for social proof, website sections, reels, stories, and paid ad creatives, depending on campaign terms and permissions.
