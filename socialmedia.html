07 FAQs

Questions about
social media marketing?

Here are the most common questions businesses ask before partnering with Scalixo Media for social media management and growth. Each answer is written to be useful on its own — what services are included, what it costs, who creates the content, which platforms we run, and how long results typically take.

Ask Your Question

What exactly does your social media marketing include?

We provide end-to-end management. This includes a custom growth strategy, monthly content planning, high-quality graphic design, video/reel editing, copywriting, daily posting, community management (replying to comments/DMs), and monthly performance reporting.

How much do social media marketing services cost?

Pricing depends on your specific goals, the number of platforms you want to manage, and the volume of content (graphics and reels) required per month. We offer custom monthly retainers tailored to your budget. Contact us for a personalized quote.

Do you create the content, or do I have to provide it?

We handle it all. Our in-house team designs the graphics, edits the short-form videos, and writes the captions. You only need to provide us with raw footage (if you want us to edit your specific videos) or basic business updates, and we take care of the rest.

Which social media platforms do you manage?

We manage Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube. We focus on platform-native strategies — such as Reels for Instagram, B2B thought leadership for LinkedIn, and Shorts for YouTube — to ensure your brand reaches the right audience on the right channel.

How long does it take to see results?

While you may see an immediate bump in engagement and profile visits within the first month, building a loyal community and generating consistent, qualified leads typically takes 3 to 6 months of strategic execution, testing, and optimization.
