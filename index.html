01 What services does Scalixo Media offer?
Scalixo Media offers six integrated growth services: social media management, performance marketing on Meta and Google, SEO and content marketing, web development, brand identity design, and influencer marketing. Each service is delivered by a specialist team and measured against business outcomes such as qualified leads, conversion rate, and return on ad spend.
02 How quickly can we expect results from social media marketing?
Most clients see meaningful lead volume within the first 30 to 60 days of a paid social campaign launch, and organic growth (follower, reach, and engagement lift) typically compounds over 3 to 6 months. SEO and content marketing usually produce measurable organic traffic lift in 3 to 6 months, with stronger results after 9 to 12 months of consistent publishing.
03 What industries does Scalixo Media work with?
Scalixo Media works with over 30 brands across real estate, D2C and retail, B2B services, SaaS, hospitality, and consumer services — primarily based in Ahmedabad and across India. We tailor channel mix, creative, and offer positioning to each industry's buyer journey and sales cycle.
04 Where is Scalixo Media located?
Scalixo Media is headquartered at 301, 3rd Floor, Harvy Complex, Subhash Chowk, Gurukul Road, Memnagar, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380052, India. We serve clients across India remotely and partner with select brands in person for kickoff, shoots, and strategy sessions.
05 How does Scalixo Media price its marketing services?
Pricing depends on the scope of services, monthly ad budget managed, and the pace of content production. Retainers typically start from a fixed monthly fee, with a separate ad-spend budget you control directly.
