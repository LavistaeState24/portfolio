Overview

What does this FAQ cover?

This FAQ covers how Scalixo Media works, what we charge, timelines, process, reporting, ownership of work, and where we operate. It is designed to answer the questions most businesses ask before starting a project with us. If you can't find your answer here, reach out directly and we'll walk you through it — we usually reply within one business day.

01

What does Scalixo Media do?

Scalixo Media is a full-service digital marketing and creative agency based in Ahmedabad. We help brands grow online through strategy, content, paid media, and performance-driven campaigns. We work as an extension of your team — planning, producing, launching, and optimising everything needed to build visibility and generate qualified demand. Learn more about our team or explore recent work.

02

Which services do you offer?

Scalixo Media offers seven core services: social media marketing and content production, performance marketing and paid advertising across Meta, Google and LinkedIn, lead generation and funnel building, search engine optimisation, website design and development, branding and creative direction, and influencer marketing. You can engage us for a single service or a fully integrated retainer.

03

How do I get started?

Getting started takes three steps. First, a discovery call where we understand your business, goals, and current challenges. Second, you receive a proposal with scope, timeline, and pricing. Third, once approved and the advance is received, we begin onboarding and kick off. Most clients go from first call to kickoff in one to two weeks.

04

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on scope, channels, deliverables, and the level of ongoing management required, so Scalixo Media quotes per project rather than publishing fixed rates. Project-based pricing applies to one-time work like websites or branding, while monthly retainers cover ongoing marketing, content and ad management. Ad spend and third-party costs such as platforms, hosting, domains and stock assets are billed separately. A non-refundable advance is required before work begins. Contact us for a scoped quote.

05

How long until I see results?

Results vary by channel and category. Paid campaigns can generate traffic and leads within days, while SEO and organic social typically need three to six months to build meaningful momentum. We set expectations upfront in the proposal and review performance against agreed milestones rather than vague promises. For SEO timelines, see Google's SEO starter guide.

06

Do you guarantee rankings or leads?

No, Scalixo Media does not guarantee rankings, traffic, leads, revenue or return on ad spend. Results depend on factors outside anyone's control — market conditions, platform algorithms, competition, pricing, and the strength of your own product or offer. What we commit to is industry best practice, transparent reporting, and continuous optimisation. This is aligned with Google's search essentials and standard marketing ethics.

07

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Scalixo Media works with early-stage startups, local businesses, D2C brands and established companies. The approach is scaled to fit your stage and budget. If your budget can't support the scope you need yet, we'll tell you honestly and suggest a leaner starting point. See our portfolio for examples across business sizes.

08

Who owns the work you create?

Once the agreed fees are paid in full, ownership of final approved deliverables created specifically for you transfers to you. Scalixo Media retains ownership of concepts, drafts, working files, internal frameworks and methodologies. Third-party assets such as fonts, stock footage and licensed software remain subject to their own licences. We may feature completed work in our portfolio unless you request otherwise in writing. Full details are in our terms.

09

How do you report on performance?

You receive a monthly report covering the metrics that matter to your goals — reach, engagement, traffic, leads, cost per result and spend — along with commentary on what worked and what we're changing next. Larger retainers can include scheduled review calls and access to live dashboards built in Google Analytics and platform-native tools.

10

Can I pause or cancel anytime?

Either party may terminate an engagement with written notice as specified in the relevant agreement. On termination, you remain responsible for fees covering work completed up to the termination date, plus any non-cancellable third-party costs already incurred on your behalf. Full details are in our terms.

11

Do you work outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Scalixo Media is based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, but works with clients across India and internationally. Most collaboration happens remotely over email, calls, and shared workspaces. In-person meetings can be arranged locally when needed. If you're outside India, see Ahmedabad for location context, or contact us to discuss timezone.
