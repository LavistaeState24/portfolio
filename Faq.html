Overview

Everything you might want to know before working with Scalixo Media — collected in one place.

Can't find your answer here? Reach out directly and we'll walk you through it. We usually reply within one business day.

01

What does Scalixo Media do?

+

Scalixo Media is a full-service digital marketing and creative agency. We help brands grow online through strategy, content, paid media, and performance-driven campaigns.

We work as an extension of your team — planning, producing, launching, and optimising everything needed to build visibility and generate qualified demand.

02

Which services do you offer?

+
  • Social media marketing and content production
  • Performance marketing and paid advertising (Meta, Google, LinkedIn)
  • Lead generation and funnel building
  • Search engine optimisation (SEO)
  • Website design and development
  • Branding, identity, and creative direction
  • Influencer marketing and collaborations

You can engage us for a single service or a fully integrated retainer.

03

How do I get started?

+

It starts with a conversation. Share your goals, current challenges, and budget range through our contact form or a quick call.

  • Discovery call — we understand your business and objectives.
  • Proposal — you receive a scope, timeline, and pricing document.
  • Onboarding — once approved and the advance is received, we kick off.
04

How much do your services cost?

+

Pricing depends on scope, channels, deliverables, and the level of ongoing management required. Because of that, we quote per project rather than publish fixed rates.

  • Project-based pricing for one-time work like websites or branding.
  • Monthly retainers for ongoing marketing, content, and ad management.
  • Ad spend and third-party costs (platforms, hosting, domains, stock assets) are billed separately.

A non-refundable advance is required before work begins, and invoices are due within the period stated on the invoice.

05

How long until I see results?

+

It varies by channel and category. Paid campaigns can generate traffic and leads within days, while SEO and organic social typically need three to six months to build meaningful momentum.

We set expectations upfront in the proposal and review performance against agreed milestones rather than vague promises.

06

Do you guarantee rankings or leads?

+

No. Digital marketing results depend on factors outside anyone's control — market conditions, platform algorithms, competition, pricing, and the strength of your own product or offer.

What we do commit to is industry best practice, transparent reporting, and continuous optimisation. We won't promise specific rankings, traffic volumes, leads, revenue, or return on ad spend.

07

Do you work with small businesses?

+

Yes. We work with early-stage startups, local businesses, D2C brands, and established companies. The approach is scaled to fit your stage and budget.

If your budget can't support the scope you need yet, we'll tell you honestly and suggest a leaner starting point.

08

Who owns the work you create?

+

Once the agreed fees are paid in full, ownership of final approved deliverables created specifically for you transfers to you, unless stated otherwise in the agreement.

Scalixo Media retains ownership of concepts, drafts, working files, internal frameworks, and methodologies. Third-party assets such as fonts, stock footage, and licensed software remain subject to their own licences.

We may feature completed work in our portfolio unless you request otherwise in writing.

09

How do you report on performance?

+

You get a monthly report covering the metrics that matter to your goals — reach, engagement, traffic, leads, cost per result, and spend — along with commentary on what worked and what we're changing next.

Larger retainers can include scheduled review calls and access to live dashboards.

10

Can I pause or cancel anytime?

+

Either party may terminate an engagement with written notice as specified in the relevant agreement.

On termination, you remain responsible for fees covering work completed up to the termination date, plus any non-cancellable third-party costs already incurred on your behalf.

11

Do you work outside Ahmedabad?

+

Absolutely. We're based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, but we work with clients across India and internationally. Most collaboration happens remotely over email, calls, and shared workspaces.

In-person meetings can be arranged locally when needed.
