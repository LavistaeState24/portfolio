05 FAQs

Questions before
working with us?

Get to know the team behind Scalixo Media. Here are the most common questions clients ask before partnering with us.

Ask Your Question

Who is Scalixo Media and what do you do?

Scalixo Media is a full-service digital marketing agency. We help businesses build their brands, scale their online presence, and generate measurable revenue through social media management, branding, SEO, web development, and performance marketing.

What makes your agency different from others?

We don't just focus on vanity metrics like likes and followers. We build systems designed for business growth. We combine high-end creative design with data-driven strategy, ensuring every piece of content or ad campaign is directly tied to your bottom line—not just looking pretty.

Where are you based, and do you work with international clients?

We are proudly headquartered in Ahmedabad, India. However, we work with clients globally across the US, UK, UAE, and Australia. Our communication, project management, and reporting processes are fully remote-friendly and designed to accommodate different time zones.

Who will I be working with on my project?

You will be assigned a dedicated Account Manager who serves as your main point of contact. They are supported by a specialized team of strategists, graphic designers, video editors, and SEO specialists depending on your service package. You will never be left guessing who to talk to.

What industries do you specialize in?

We have deep experience across a wide range of sectors including real estate, healthcare, e-commerce, hospitality, fashion, and B2B professional services. Our core strategies are universal, but we always take the time to research and understand the unique nuances of your specific industry before launching a campaign.

Do you require long-term contracts?

For project-based work (like web design or branding), we work on a fixed-scope basis. For ongoing services (like SEO or social media management), we typically recommend a 3 to 6-month initial commitment to allow strategies to take effect and show tangible results. After that, we operate on rolling monthly agreements.
