Here are the most common questions businesses ask before starting their branding and identity project with Scalixo Media — covering what's included, how long it takes, pricing, revisions, editable files, and rebranding. Ask Your Question

What is included in branding and identity design?

It includes brand strategy, logo design, visual identity (color palette, typography), brand messaging, brand guidelines, and marketing collaterals. You get a complete brand system, not just a standalone logo — so your business looks professional, communicates clearly, and stays consistent everywhere, from your website and ads to packaging and social media.

Do you only design logos?

No. We build complete identity systems, not just logos. A logo is one element; an identity is the entire system that element lives in — including typography, color palette, graphic elements, tone of voice, and usage rules. We deliver all of it together so your brand stays consistent everywhere your customers see it.

How long does a branding project take?

Most branding projects take 3 to 8 weeks depending on scope. A full identity system with strategy, messaging, and brand guidelines typically takes 4 to 8 weeks; a focused logo refresh can be completed in 2 to 3 weeks. Timeline depends on the number of deliverables, feedback turnaround, and revision rounds.

How much does branding cost?

Pricing depends on scope, not just hours. A logo-only project costs significantly less than a full identity system with strategy, messaging, and brand guidelines. We provide a clear, itemized quote based on your specific deliverables — no hidden fees, no surprise line items, and no paying for services you don't need.

Can you rebrand my existing business?

Yes. We can refresh or fully redesign your existing brand while keeping the parts that already work for your audience. If your positioning is right but the visual execution is dated or inconsistent, a refresh is often enough; if your market or promise has fundamentally changed, a full rebrand is the better path. We'll recommend the right scope after reviewing your current brand.

Will I receive editable files?

Yes. We provide editable source files (AI, Figma, PSD) and final exports (PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF) so your team can use your identity across every platform. Full ownership of the final files is transferred to you upon project completion — no licensing fees, no ongoing charges.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds are included in every project based on scope. We typically include two to three rounds of refinements so you can fine-tune the identity without worrying about extra costs. Additional rounds beyond the included scope are always quoted upfront before any work begins.
