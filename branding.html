07 FAQs

Questions before
building your brand?

Here are common questions businesses ask before starting their branding and identity project with Scalixo Media.

Ask Your Question

What is included in branding and identity design?

It includes brand strategy, logo design, visual identity, color palette, typography, brand messaging, social templates, and brand guidelines. You get a complete system, not just a standalone logo.

Do you only design logos?

No. We build complete identity systems, not just logos. A logo is one element; an identity is the system that element lives in—including typography, color, graphic elements, and usage rules to keep your brand consistent everywhere.[reference:0]

How long does a branding project take?

Most branding projects take 3 to 8 weeks depending on scope. A full identity system with strategy, messaging, and brand guidelines typically takes 4 to 8 weeks; a focused logo refresh can be completed in 2 to 3 weeks.[reference:1]

How much does branding cost?

Pricing depends on scope, not just hours. A logo-only project costs significantly less than a full identity system with strategy, messaging, and brand guidelines. We provide a clear, itemized quote based on your specific deliverables—no hidden fees.[reference:3][reference:4]

Can you rebrand my existing business?

Yes. We can refresh or fully redesign your existing brand while keeping the parts that already work for your audience. If your positioning is right but the visual execution is dated or inconsistent, a refresh is often enough; if your market or promise has fundamentally changed, a full rebrand is the better path.[reference:5]

Will I receive editable files?

Yes. We provide editable source files and final exports so your team can use your identity across every platform. Full ownership of the final files is transferred to you upon project completion.[reference:6][reference:7]

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds are included in every project based on the scope. We typically include two to three rounds of refinements so you can fine-tune the identity without worrying about extra costs.[reference:8][reference:9]
