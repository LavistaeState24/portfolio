02

Information We Collect

Scalixo Media collects two categories of data: (1) personal information you provide directly — name, email, phone, company name, business details, website URL, and project requirements; and (2) technical information collected automatically — IP address, browser type, device data, operating system, pages visited, session duration, and cookies. We collect only what is needed to respond to inquiries, quote projects, and deliver services.

04

Cookies

Scalixo Media uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep the site functioning, remember preferences, measure traffic via Google Analytics, and support marketing campaigns via Meta Pixel and Google Ads. Cookies fall into three categories: functional, analytics, and marketing. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; some site features may not work correctly without them.

05

Information Security

Scalixo Media applies industry-standard safeguards — encrypted transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and secure hosting — to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. No method of online transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described, and we review our safeguards periodically.

Scalixo Media works with trusted third-party providers to deliver services. 07

Sharing Information

Scalixo Media does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only when: (1) required by law or legal process; (2) necessary to deliver a service you requested; or (3) needed to protect our legal rights or prevent fraud. Every third-party processor we engage is bound by confidentiality obligations and is permitted to use your data only for the purpose we specify.

Scalixo Media, 301, 3rd Floor, Harvy Complex, Gurukul Rd, Memnagar, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380052, is the data controller for personal information collected on scalixomedia.com.
